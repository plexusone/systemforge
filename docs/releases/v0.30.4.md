# v0.30.4

**Release Date:** 2026-11-02

Signing out can land on the product's own site:

- **`social_login.signed_out_url`.** A product's apps each returned a
  signed-out browser to their own origin, which showed the sign-in page
  again. When `signed_out_url` is set, every sign-out lands there instead,
  such as the product's marketing site, whatever `return_to` the app sent.

It adds no breaking change: without the setting, sign-out returns where it
did.

## Upgrade notes

1. **To land sign-outs on a marketing site,** add its origin to
   `social_login.allowed_redirect_origins` and set
   `social_login.signed_out_url` to its page. A URL outside the allowed
   origins is refused at startup.

See [Social Login](../systemauth/social-login.md).

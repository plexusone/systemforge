    Billing¶

    Package billing takes payments through payforge. The products and prices come from a pricing spec; payforge creates them at Stripe, starts checkout and the customer portal, and turns what Stripe reports into entitlements. billing supplies the storage and the endpoints, on the SystemForge database.

    The organization pays. A person starts a checkout or opens the portal on behalf of an organization they may manage, and the plan or license belongs to the organization.

    What it does¶

    Stripe reports SystemForge stores
    A subscription to a plan of the spec, created, changed or ended The organization's platform subscription (sf_subscriptions row with no listing): plan_tier is the plan the organization is on, the spec's default plan when nothing grants
    A paid one-time checkout for a listing A license for the buying organization
    A full refund of that checkout The license suspended, for good
    A dispute of that payment The license suspended while the dispute is open or once it is lost; restored when it is won
    A seller's connected account changing Whether the seller can take payments, which decides whether its paid listings can be published

    Every change writes the authorization graph again, whether or not the database changed, so a graph write that failed is repaired when Stripe redelivers the event. Events are recorded once processed (sf_billing_events), so a redelivered event applies once. Events of another app sharing the Stripe account, of the other mode (test or live), or from a seller's own account are acknowledged and not applied.

    Wiring¶

    import (
    "github.com/plexusone/payforge"

    "github.com/plexusone/systemforge/billing"
    "github.com/plexusone/systemforge/billing/pglock"
    "github.com/plexusone/systemforge/marketplace"
)

spec, err := pricingspec.Load(specFile)

// One lock per organization, held across instances. On a single instance,
// &payforge.MemoryLocker{} also works.
locker := pglock.New(sqlDB, logger)

store := billing.NewStore(entClient)
sink := billing.NewSink(entClient,
    billing.WithAuthzSyncer(marketplace.NewSpiceDBSyncer(spice)),
    billing.OnEntitlement(func(ctx context.Context, c billing.PlanChange) error {
        return nil // update state of your own that follows the plan
    }))

client, reader, err := billing.NewStripe(spec, billing.StripeConfig{
    SecretKey:       os.Getenv("STRIPE_SECRET_KEY"),
    WebhookSecrets:  []string{platformSecret, connectSecret},
    AllowedURLHosts: []string{"app.example.com"},
    Locker:          locker,
})

life := &payforge.Lifecycle{
    Spec: spec, Store: store, Sink: sink,
    Sales: sink, Sellers: sink, Disputes: sink,
    Provider: client, Accounts: client, Locker: locker,
}
hook, err := billing.NewWebhookHandler(billing.WebhookConfig{
    Reader: reader, Lifecycle: life, Store: store, Checkouts: sink, Logger: logger,
})
mux.Handle("POST /billing/webhook", hook)

co, err := billing.NewCheckout(billing.CheckoutConfig{
    Billing: client, Connect: client, Spec: spec, Store: store, DB: entClient,
    Listings: svc.Listings(), ListingProduct: "listing",
    Purchases: marketplace.OrgRolePurchaseAuthorizer(orgAuthorizer),
    Locker:    locker,
    URLs: billing.RedirectURLs{
        PlanSuccess:    "https://app.example.com/billing/done",
        PlanCancel:     "https://app.example.com/pricing",
        ListingSuccess: "https://app.example.com/market/done",
        ListingCancel:  "https://app.example.com/market",
        PortalReturn:   "https://app.example.com/billing",
    },
    AllowedURLHosts: []string{"app.example.com"},
    Logger:          logger,
})
co.Register(mux, "/billing")

// Paid listings can be published only by sellers that can take payments.
svc := marketplace.NewEntService(entClient, syncer).WithPublishGate(billing.NewSellerGate(store))

    Without a Stripe key, use payforge.Disabled(spec): every organization is on the spec's default plan and checkout answers 501.

    Before taking payments, sync the catalog to Stripe once with the client's Sync (see payforge's catalog guide), and register the webhook endpoint for the events payforge reads.

    Endpoints¶

    Endpoint Body Answer
    POST /billing/checkout/plan {"plan": "pro", "interval": "month"} {"url": ...} of the checkout
    POST /billing/checkout/listing {"listing_id": "..."} {"url": ...} of the checkout
    POST /billing/portal none {"url": ...} of the customer portal
    POST /billing/webhook Stripe's event ok
    • The organization comes from the caller's credential (requestprincipal.RequestPrincipal.Organization); set CheckoutConfig.Organization to resolve it another way.
    • The caller must pass Purchases for the organization. NewCheckout refuses a configuration without one.
    • The Stripe customer is found from the organization, never from the request, and created the first time.
    • Redirect URLs are configured, never taken from the request, and must be https on a host in AllowedURLHosts. With a test-mode Stripe key, localhost and other loopback hosts also pass, over http too, for local development; with a live key they are refused when the checkout is built.
    • Errors are fixed messages, {"error": ...}; causes are logged.

    The webhook answers 400 to a signature that does not verify, 413 to a body over 1 MiB, 500 when handling failed so Stripe redelivers, and 200 otherwise.

    Listings¶

    Only one-time listings are sold through checkout: payforge sells subscriptions to the spec's plans, not to individual listings. A listing is sold as the spec's seller-set product named by ListingProduct, at the listing's price. With a marketplace section in the spec, the sale goes to the seller's connected account and the platform keeps its fee; record the account with store.LinkSeller after payforge's Connect.CreateAccount. The checkout is recorded (sf_billing_orders) before the buyer sees it, and its payment, refunds and disputes are matched through that record.

    Tables¶

    Created by the Ent schema migration (systemauth migrate, or Schema.Create); no SQL migration is needed.

    Table Holds
    sf_billing_events Processed event IDs; prune with Store.PruneEvents
    sf_billing_customers Stripe customer of each organization
    sf_billing_orders Listing checkouts and the license each granted
    sf_billing_refunds Highest refunded total per charge
    sf_billing_disputes Disputes of charges
    sf_billing_sellers Sellers' connected accounts

    Upgrading from marketplace/stripe¶

    marketplace/stripe, marketplace.CheckoutService and the marketplace API's POST /checkout endpoint are removed, with the dependency on github.com/stripe/stripe-go/v84.

    1. Write a pricing spec for the plans and products you sell, and sync it to Stripe. Prices are found by lookup key; a price created by hand before the spec grants nothing unless listed in payforge.Lifecycle.LegacyPrices.
    2. Replace stripe.NewCheckoutService and EntService.WithCheckout with the wiring above, and mount billing's endpoints. Remove marketplace.WithPurchaseAuthorizer; pass the authorizer as CheckoutConfig.Purchases.
    3. Clients call POST /billing/checkout/listing with listing_id only, in place of POST /checkout. The organization comes from the credential, and success_url and cancel_url are configured on the server.
    4. Point the Stripe webhook endpoint at the new handler; there was none before. Add the events payforge reads: checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded, checkout.session.async_payment_failed, customer.subscription.*, charge.refunded, charge.dispute.created, charge.dispute.closed, and, with a marketplace, account.updated on the Connect endpoint.
    5. Run the schema migration to create the sf_billing_* tables.
    6. Listings sold by subscription or per seat cannot be bought through the new checkout. Licenses granted for them earlier are unchanged; sell subscriptions as plans of the spec.
